## F1: Autopilot task_status signaling is fragile — QA/Reflect silently skipped on merged work

**Frequency:** 6+ incidents across 3 failure modes (cross-session reinforcement).
**Evidence:**
- `project_qa-skip-background-notification-hijack` (2026-06-28): 3 of 6 awardybot
  specs (BUG-1313/1311/1309) skipped QA. Root: autopilot runs pytest in background,
  final turn responds to `<task-notification>` instead of emitting
  `task_status: "complete"` JSON. Callback Step 6 allowlist sees `task_status=''`
  and silently skips QA+Reflect dispatch. Lifecycle still closes `done` via guard.
- `project_autopilot-failed-but-merged` (2026-06-20): pueue `Failed` (exit 1) but
  work merged on develop. Guard closes done, but callback never dispatches QA on
  `Failed` status — only on `done AND task_status==complete`.
- `project_init-timeout-transient-block` (2026-06-27/28): 3 specs (FTR-1300,
  BUG-1309, BUG-1310) crashed at `turns=0/$0` ("Control request timeout:
  initialize"), false-blocked without work. Recovery = operator demote + re-dispatch.

**Type:** failure_pattern (the load-bearing QA gate has 3 independent bypass paths).

**Existing spec:** TECH-207 (queued, founder-confirmed 2026-07-12) addresses the
background-notification root cause. But the PATTERN is wider: any disruption to the
final-turn `task_status: "complete"` emission silences the entire post-autopilot
tail (QA + Reflect). The init-timeout and Failed-but-merged paths are NOT in scope
of TECH-207.

**Proposed action:**
- TECH-207 fixes the acute root (background notification hijack). Additionally:
- claude-runner should scan ALL messages for `task_status`, not just the final one
  (TECH-207 Layer A already proposes this).
- callback Step 6 should dispatch QA when guard confirmed merge on develop, EVEN
  without explicit `task_status==complete` (fallback path, TECH-207 Layer B).
- Init-timeout (`turns=0`) should auto-retry in claude-runner instead of marking
  Failed (separate TECH candidate).

**Risk if ignored:** P0 production fixes silently bypass QA — BUG-1313 (Pampers
delivery-date validator) merged with zero behavioral testing. Trust erosion.

  forever. 9 push-failures + 9 "skip cycle" in 24h on awardybot.

  concurrency-cancels running CI workflow (GitHub Actions `cancel-in-progress`),
  preventing migration apply. Separate root but same source: callback push
  timing vs. CI.

scenario differently: callback has rebase-recovery, orchestrator does not).

**Proposed action:**
